Texts of the Bible

The quotations and verse texts in each language come from a public-domain edition of the Bible in that language; the electronic texts are from eBible.org and from CrossWire’s SWORD library. Names and the existence of verses are checked against the Synodal translation and the King James Version, and the number of verses in each chapter against the World English Bible (public domain, eBible.org). Where an edition numbers a chapter otherwise than the KJV, the card leaves its verses out and keeps the link. In the Gdańsk Bible one word for the Cushites, offensive today, is replaced by „Etiopczyk”, as in modern Polish Bibles. The Bíblia Portuguesa Mundial is a draft translation in the public domain, still being revised by its translators.

Photographs

The {{photos}} photographs of places come from Wikimedia Commons, and only those in the public domain or under CC0 or CC BY are used. The author, licence and a link to the file are given under each photograph; for a disputed place the caption says which proposed site it shows. Photographs are resized and cropped to fit the frame.
