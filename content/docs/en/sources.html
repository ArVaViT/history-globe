Texts of the Bible

The Russian Synodal translation (1876), the World English Bible and the King James Version, all in the public domain; the electronic texts are from eBible.org. Names and the existence of verses are checked against them; quotations in the tours and verse texts come from the Synodal translation and the King James Version.

Photographs

The {{photos}} photographs of places come from Wikimedia Commons, and only those in the public domain or under CC0 or CC BY are used. The author, licence and a link to the file are given under each photograph; for a disputed place the caption says which proposed site it shows. Photographs are resized and cropped to fit the frame.

The site uses MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3-Clause), React (MIT), Lucide (ISC) and Feather (MIT) icons, and the Golos Text and Literata typefaces (SIL Open Font License). Their licence texts: third-party.txt, lucide-icons.txt; MapLibre’s is shipped with its files.
