# Checklist (use any of it, or none)

For each article:

- [ ] Is the place identified correctly? Is the identification stated with the right confidence
      (certain / probable / disputed)?
- [ ] Are the dates right, and is their basis clear (ancient author, inscription, archaeology,
      convention)?
- [ ] Is anything stated as fact that is really an opinion or a disputed reading?
- [ ] Is an important view, find or recent publication missing?
- [ ] Are the cited sources the right ones, cited correctly, and not outdated?
- [ ] Do the scripture references support what the text says?
- [ ] Is the map position plausible? (optional)
- [ ] Anything that would embarrass a teacher who used this in class?

For the public record (we draft it from your reply; you approve or rewrite):

- [ ] Your name, title and institution as you want them shown
- [ ] What you reviewed for, and what you did not (e.g. "not the translations")
- [ ] A short statement in your own words (2–5 sentences)
- [ ] Conflicts of interest, or "none"
- [ ] Full review public? yes / no — DOI on Zenodo? yes / no
- [ ] Link: ORCID / university page — Photo: none
