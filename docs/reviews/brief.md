# Reviewing History Globe — brief

Thank you for agreeing to look at History Globe (https://historyglobe.app), a free 3D atlas of
biblical history in 16 languages. It has no ads, accounts or income, and will stay free.

**What you are reading.** <N> articles on <scope>, in the version of <date> (commit <abc1234>).
Links and a PDF with numbered paragraphs are attached.

**How the texts were made.** Each article was drafted with AI help under my direction from the
sources it cites, then checked claim by claim against those sources in AI-assisted passes (the
last completed on 17 October 2026: 9,775 claims, 1,088 corrections). No scholar has read them
yet. Methodology:
https://historyglobe.app/docs/methodology.html

**What helps most.**

- Errors of fact: identifications, dates, distances, who did what.
- Claims stated more firmly than the evidence allows, or a scholarly debate left out.
- Sources that are outdated, misread or missing, or better ones to cite.

Please ignore style unless it misleads.

**How to reply.** Whatever is easiest: a few lines in an email, notes on the PDF, or tracked
changes in Word. Please name the article and paragraph ("Colossae ¶3"). Short is fine. No account,
form or software is needed.

**Time.** About <1 / 3> hours. Any date that suits you; <suggested date> is only a suggestion.

**What happens next.**

1. Within two weeks I answer every point and correct the text in all 16 languages.
2. You may check the response and tell me what is not addressed.
3. I send you the exact public text: your name, title, institution, what you reviewed and what you
   did not, your statement in your own words, and a conflict-of-interest line.
4. Nothing is published until you reply "Approved". Reviews are signed with your name; if you
   would rather not be named, you are free to decline. You may publish only a short statement,
   or withdraw at any time before publication. After publication I take a review off the site at
   your request; earlier versions stay in the public history of the repository, as every change
   does.

**What you receive.** Your name on each article you reviewed and on the Reviews page, linked to
your page or ORCID; your review published under CC BY 4.0 with you as its author; if you wish, a
DOI for it on Zenodo, which you can add to your ORCID record and CV; and a letter listing what
changed because of your review.

**What it does not mean.** You review only these articles, in your personal capacity. It is not an
endorsement of the site, and your institution is named for identification only.

Review policy: https://historyglobe.app/docs/reviews.html
