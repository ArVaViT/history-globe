# Open legal questions

To be answered by a lawyer before commercial launch. Collected from a two-pass
licence review on 2026-09-28 (ADR 0008). None of these blocks the MVP; each marks a
source we either avoid or use in a way that stays safe whatever the answer is.

1. **STEPBible TIPNR** (CC BY 4.0) — are the README phrases "please do not redistribute
   it yourself", "without changing the data" and "changes ... should be made available to
   subsequent users" binding conditions or requests?
2. **Copernicus DEM** — how to pass the obligations of Article 6(e) on to licensees, and
   what the indemnity in Article 7 means for us.
3. **Mapterhorn, Israel 10 m model** (ASTER, processed by 4cast LTD) — does 4cast hold
   rights or impose terms?
4. **KJV in the United Kingdom** (Crown copyright) — is an agreement with Cambridge needed
   for a digital product that shows full verses to UK users?
5. **CCEL terms** — do they bind us when the same public-domain text is obtained from
   another source (CrossWire, Wikisource)?
6. **Google scans on the Internet Archive** — legal weight of the "non-commercial use"
   request attached to public-domain scans.
7. **OpenBible** — are the ~51 coordinates derived from OpenStreetMap an "insubstantial
   part" under ODbL?
8. **Cliopatria** — polygons were redrawn from printed atlases: residual risk of claims by
   the atlas publishers.
9. **Russian Synodal text** — does the Russian Bible Society hold rights in the modern
   edited text we would display?
10. **Source snapshots** — may we keep private full copies of copyrighted sources for
    internal fact-checking, or only URLs, hashes and short excerpts?
